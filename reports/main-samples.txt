===== sample 1 =====
Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the garden. One day, she saw a pretty butterfly flying around. She wanted to catch it, so she ran after it. But the butterfly flew away too fast.
Lily's mommy called her inside for dinner. She had made some spicy chicken for dinner. Lily loved spicy food, so she tried a little bit. It was too spicy for her! She drank some water to cool down.
After dinner, Lily went back outside to look for the butterfly. She searched and searched, but couldn't find it. She decided to sit down and watch the sunset. The sky turned pink and orange, and it was so beautiful. Lily felt happy and peaceful, just like the butterfly she saw earlier.

===== sample 2 =====
Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and her favorite toy was a teddy bear named Brownie. Brownie was her best friend and she always made sure he was safe and happy.
One day, Lily's mom came into her room and said, "Lily, it's time for your nap. Let's go to your room and rest." Lily didn't want to take a nap because she was having so much fun playing with her toys. But her mom said, "I'll leave you alone, sweetie. You can play with your toys later."
Lily went to her room and laid down on her bed. She hugged Brownie and closed her eyes. Suddenly, she heard a noise. It sounded like a big monster! She got scared and called out, "Brownie, what's that noise?" Brownie replied, "Don't worry, Lily. I'll protect you."
The noise got closer and closer. Lily closed her eyes again, but this time it was too late. The monster came into her room and ate Brownie up. Lily was so sad that she cried and cried. She learned that sometimes it's better to have a nap when you're scared.

But then, Lily saw a big bird flying in the sky. She thought it was scary, but it was fun to watch. She forgot about playing with the toy car and went home with her mom. They had a good day at the park. The end.
